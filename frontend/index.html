Comprehensive Grievance Categories

Every department and operational facility across the campus is monitored with strict SLA resolution timelines.

Academics & Teaching

Course curriculum delivery, syllabus schedules, faculty responsiveness, lecture overlaps, and internal evaluation marks.

Labs & Classrooms

Projectors, lab workstations, specialized test equipment, ventilation, air conditioning, and safety gear.

Hostels & Dining

Room maintenance, hot water supply, mess food hygiene, drinking water purifiers, and residential warden support.

IT & Campus Internet

High-speed campus Wi-Fi access points, student portal authentication, library digital repositories, and email accounts.

Safety & Wellbeing

Campus pathway lighting, security patrols, emergency first aid, medical infirmary, and anti-harassment protection.

Certificates & Admin

Bona fide certificates, official transcripts, scholarship verification, fee receipts, and bus transport passes.
