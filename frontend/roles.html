Institutional Role Archetypes

Student

Submits grievances, uploads evidence, tracks personal cases, posts responses, and rates redressal satisfaction.

Faculty / Staff

Investigates complaints assigned to them, adds progress updates, updates status to In Progress, and posts public responses.

Department HOD

Oversees department cases, assigns cases to faculty, records internal notes, and monitors department SLA performance.

Grievance Officer

Central investigative authority. Full access to all standard and confidential grievances. Marks official resolutions.

System Administrator

Manages user accounts, system configuration, categories, and audit logs. Configures RBAC permissions.

Quality Auditor

Read-only compliance auditor. Inspects non-confidential reports, resolution timelines, and system audit trails.
